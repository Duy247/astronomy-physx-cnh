Unprocessed Image Files
New image sets posted - December, 2016.
Available for downloading are Unprocessed FITS Image Files for a number of sets of images taken with the 32" Ritchey-Chretien telescope. There is no charge for use of this data, though it will only be of use to those who are familiar with the processing of astronomical image data. This page also has links to image comparison pages which display different versions of several of the images as processed by separate imagers.

There are also a number of image sets available which were taken with Steve Mazlin's 4" Takahashi FSQ106N from my Arizona observatory in 2006. These are listed at the bottom of the FITS file download page.

here. The tutorials start with FITS files available here, and proceed

Commentary
I have enjoyed amateur astronomy as an interesting and educational hobby for over 50 years. I have owned many telescopes over the years, beginning with a low-end Sears 3" Refractor in about 1960. I built a 6" Newtownian, including mirror grinding/polishing, while in high school. Soon after college I built telescopes using 12 1/2" and 17 1/2" inch mirrors from Coulter Optical, and owned a Criterion Dynamax 8.

A little over 25 years ago I bought a 30" Dobsonian made by Tectron Telescopes, and at about the same time bought a Meade 178ED 7" refractor. These were the first telescopes I used at my Northwestern Arizona location, where I built a home in 1992.

A few years later I was able to place an order for a 32" Ritchey-Chretien telescope, and it was delivered in February of 2001. The time requirements of my employment kept me from getting this instrument ready for astrophotography until March of 2003, at which time I began taking astrophotos using a 35mm camera and slide film. In May of 2004, I purchased an SBIG STL-11000m ccd camera, and used it with the 32" scope until 2006.

I no longer have the 32" RC scope or the 7" refractor, and haven't done any imaging since 2006.

I currently have a Meade ETX125 scope and a Coronado 90 solar scope, which I take out on occasion in my back yard in eastern Pennsylvania.

- Jim Misti
